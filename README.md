# Yousef Al Arif

I'm building [Popcorn AI](https://trypopcorn.ai): AI agents that sell, support and market for online stores on WhatsApp.

Popcorn AI connects to a store's Shopify, Zid or Salla catalog, orders and delivery, then talks to its customers on WhatsApp: it answers product questions, recommends products, takes orders and payment in the chat, and follows up.

Stack: TypeScript, Node.js, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, Next.js and React Native on Google Cloud.

Based in Dubai and San Francisco.
